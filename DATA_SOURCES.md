# Data provenance and attribution

1. **Flor et al. cognitive-aging study**: DOI `10.1101/2025.07.13.662751`; data DOI `10.5281/zenodo.15849341`. The original `Ensembl2GeneSymbol.csv` and six Boruta feature-frequency summaries are reproduced in `reference/boruta/`. The full download also extracts the original `DATA_LICENSE.txt`. Molecular matrices remain in the original archive and are not bundled.
2. **Ensembl release 116, Mus musculus GRCm39**: transcript/gene mapping and selected-gene symbols used for tensor construction and Enrichr. The GTF is downloaded at runtime. `reference/boruta/ENSMUST00000239425.json` records the one mapping supplement used in the original Boruta comparison.
3. **Drokhlyansky et al. (2020)**: DOI `10.1016/j.cell.2020.08.003`; article `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8358727/`. The supplied Table S4 spreadsheet is bundled unchanged as `reference/ens/Drokhlyansky_Table_S4.xlsx`. The analysis uses `Mouse Colon All`, not another sheet. The two short signature lists are transcribed from STAR Methods, “Acquisition and scoring of gene signatures”; the article attributes these lists to Lasrado et al. (2017).
4. **Enrichr**: `https://maayanlab.cloud/Enrichr/`; Xie et al. (2021), DOI `10.1002/cpz1.90`. Archived library exports, input symbols and metadata are in `reference/enrichr/`. The original all-library run finished on 2026-10-06 UTC. Frozen GO Biological Process 2026 and PanglaoDB Augmented 2021 GMT files were used for the separate local Boruta-excluded analysis. “Augmented” must be retained when describing the cell-type library.
5. **Derived analysis inputs/results**: the 75-individual map, matched behavioral table, molecular component scores, 16,080-gene background, 1,577 selected IDs/symbols, correlation reference results, ENS reference tests, and Boruta-excluded reference results are the supplied manuscript-analysis artifacts. They are included to allow explicit checks against the published analysis; they are not independent experimental data.

See `reference/MD5SUMS.csv` for file-integrity checks. Checksums identify this packaged snapshot; they do not certify source authenticity. Source datasets, annotations, and supplementary material retain their original terms and attribution. A code license should be chosen by the repository owner before release; no license is assumed for the owner's unpublished code.
